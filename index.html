03 Inside the plant

Every step,
under one roof.

Cutting, printing, cover placement, stitching and trimming run on automated lines at our HSIIDC unit. It means tighter tolerances, honest lead times and a price that comes straight from the floor.

  • OEM and private label — your logo, cover and packaging
  • Export documentation and palletised packing
  • Bulk pricing for distributors, schools and CSR buyers
  • Made in India, 100% in-house production
Book a factory visit
  1. 01

    Paper & board

    Reels and sheets selected by GSM, brightness and ruling; cover board matched to the run.

  2. 02

    Print & laminate

    Cover artwork printed four-colour, laminated matte or gloss, then cut to register.

  3. 03

    Bind & trim

    Stitch, spiral or wiro binding on automated lines, trimmed square and checked in-line.

  4. 04

    Pack & dispatch

    Shrink-wrapped in dozens, cartons marked for the channel, loaded for Delhi or the port.
